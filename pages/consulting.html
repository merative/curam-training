Cúram Education Consultancy Services

Analyzing Your Unique Needs

Cúram Education offers a training consultancy service that performs training needs analysis to identify skills-gaps of your project teams and offers training solutions to bridge the skills-gaps within the budget, priorities, and timelines of your project.

The result of the analysis is a training plan that can include recommended training courses, custom courseware, and even mentoring to help your project teams acquire the necessary skills to successfully implement Cúram solutions.

Custom Learning Paths

If analysis reveals that our existing Learning Paths do not fully address your needs, we can develop custom paths tailored to your specific requirements.

The custom learning path may be created using just existing courses, or include new custom courseware, as described in the next section.

Custom Training Courseware

Based on the outcome of the analysis, we can create custom training materials to address any learning needs or scenarios unique to your organization. This can include custom presentations, exercises, and labs that are designed to help your teams acquire the necessary skills to successfully implement Cúram solutions.

Cúram Certification Mentoring

Mentoring programs are available to provide personalized guidance and support as you work towards earning your Cúram Certification. Cúram Certification Mentoring Programs consist of the following components:

  • Analysis of Practice and Certification Test Results. After taking a Practice or Certification test, you receive a spreadsheet that lists the specific pages in the training that you need to revise based on your test performance.
  • Hands-On Mentoring – This service consists of one-to-one sessions with Cúram trainers who can provide comprehensive, detailed certification advice.

For Developers, there are two different sessions available:

  • Accreditation Exam Mentoring
  • Certification Exam Mentoring

In both cases, the sessions offer expert guidance from experienced Cúram trainers, interactive question and answer activities, and targeted instruction based on learner readiness, feedback and individual needs. To find out more about this program, click Cúram_Developer_Certification_Mentoring

And one flavor of Business Analyst Mentoring is available:

  • Business Exam Mentoring

The session is designed to help the student develop effective preparation strategies for the test and includes interactive review sessions to reinforce key concepts, answer questions, and check candidate readiness. To find out more about this program, click Cúram_BA_Certification_Mentoring
